Common questions

Things people
usually ask.

What happens after I send this?

You’ll get a personal reply (not a templated one) within 24 hours. If it looks like a fit, I propose a 30-minute discovery call. If it doesn’t, I’ll tell you and point you somewhere useful.

Do I have to know my budget?

No, but it helps. Even a rough number lets me suggest the right tier. “Not sure” is a fine answer — we’ll figure it out on the call.

One-time project or monthly retainer?

Most builds are a one-time project. After launch, an optional retainer keeps the site updated, monitored, and edited. Pick “Not sure” and I’ll recommend what fits.

Can we meet in person?

Yes, if you’re near Tilburg, Eindhoven or Breda. Otherwise we work over video and shared docs — fine for 95% of projects.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, mutual NDAs before discovery if your project needs it. Just mention it in the message.
