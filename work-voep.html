VOEPE-commerce · Tier III

Sixty years
of parts.
Now a
real shop.

A hand-built WooCommerce store for VOEP, a family outboard-parts business going since 1964. Thousands of rare parts across five engine brands, bilingual, and selling worldwide.

ClientVOEP · Duinker Motor Service
ScopeTier III · WooCommerce
Year2026
ResultLive · First sale (Sweden)
The brief

VOEP has sold outboard-engine parts since 1964, a family trade that ran from Texel in the Netherlands to Paal-Beringen in Belgium. New, original, and quality-used parts for Mercury, Yamaha, Suzuki, Honda and Johnson/Evinrude.

Alex, the owner, is a mechanic of fifty years, not a web person. He needed a real shop: one that could carry thousands of rare parts and sell them worldwide.

The challenge

A deep parts catalog, made shoppable.

Thousands of SKUs across five engine brands, where the buyer is a mechanic hunting one exact part. It had to be searchable, sell across borders, take real payments, and stay legal in the EU, without looking like a stock template.

The approach

A real storefront,
hand‑built for scale.

01

Built for the catalog

A WooCommerce store on a custom theme, structured for thousands of parts across five brands, with SKU-and-name search so a mechanic finds the exact part in seconds.

02

Sells across borders

Bilingual EN and NL, EU-compliant checkout with card, Bancontact and iDeal, and worldwide shipping. Its first real order shipped to Sweden.

03

Heritage, not a template

Hand-coded, no page-builder bloat. A storefront that carries the 1964 family story instead of a generic shop skin, marine blueprints and all.

The work

Five brands,
one shop.

The outcome

Live and selling. The first real order shipped to Sweden within days of launch, and the store is built to carry the full four-thousand-plus part catalog as the data lands.
