General Terms & Conditions

The ground rules for working together. Noxta Studio contracts with business clients (B2B), under Dutch law.

Noxta Studio · Version 1.0 · 29 May 2026

Article 1 — Definitions

  • Noxta Studio: Adrian Tajti, trading as Noxta Studio, established in Tilburg, the Netherlands. KVK 42092474.
  • Client: the natural or legal person who enters into an agreement with Noxta Studio.
  • Agreement: the contract between Noxta Studio and the Client to which these conditions apply.
  • Services: all work performed by Noxta Studio, including but not limited to web design, web development, branding and consulting.
  • Deliverables: the digital files, designs, code or other outputs delivered to the Client.

Article 2 — Applicability

2.1 These conditions apply to every quotation, agreement and Service between Noxta Studio and the Client.
2.2 Deviating terms apply only if expressly agreed in writing.
2.3 The Client’s own general terms are explicitly rejected.
2.4 These conditions are made available to the Client by reference in every quotation issued by Noxta Studio and are published at noxta.studio/terms. The Client confirms receipt by accepting the quotation in writing (including by email).
2.5 Noxta Studio contracts with business clients (B2B) exclusively. Consumer (B2C) engagements are not offered. Where a Client is a natural person acting outside their trade or profession, no Agreement is formed unless Noxta Studio has expressly agreed otherwise in writing.

Article 3 — Quotations and agreement

3.1 All quotations are non-binding and valid for 30 days from the date of issue, unless stated otherwise.
3.2 The Agreement is formed when the Client confirms the quotation in writing (including by email) or when Noxta Studio begins performance at the Client’s request.
3.3 Obvious errors in a quotation do not bind Noxta Studio.

Article 4 — Performance of the Services

4.1 Noxta Studio will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care on a best-efforts basis. Unless explicitly agreed in writing, all deadlines are indicative.
4.2 The Client provides all materials, content, access and feedback needed to perform the Services in a timely manner.
4.3 Delay caused by the Client extends Noxta Studio’s deadlines and may increase the agreed price.

Article 5 — Changes and additional work

5.1 Changes to the agreed scope are only carried out after written agreement on the new scope and any additional fee.
5.2 The quotation includes one round of revisions per design stage unless otherwise specified. Further revisions are billed at the applicable hourly rate.

Article 6 — Prices and payment

6.1 All prices are in euros and exclusive of BTW (VAT) and third-party costs (e.g. hosting, domain, fonts, stock images, plugins), unless stated otherwise.
6.2 Unless otherwise agreed, Noxta Studio invoices 50% of the project fee at the start and 50% on delivery. Maintenance and hourly work are invoiced monthly.
6.2.1 Maintenance retainers are monthly. Either party may cancel with 30 days’ written notice (email is sufficient). Unused edit hours do not roll forward beyond the retainer’s stated limit (e.g. 2 months for the Light plan) and are not refundable on cancellation. Annual prepay arrangements, where offered, are non-refundable once paid.
6.3 Invoices are payable within 14 days of the invoice date.
6.4 If the Client fails to pay on time, the Client is in default without further notice and owes statutory commercial interest (Article 6:119a Dutch Civil Code) and extrajudicial collection costs in accordance with the Decree on Compensation for Extrajudicial Collection Costs.
6.5 Noxta Studio may suspend Services and withhold delivery while invoices remain unpaid.

Article 7 — Delivery and acceptance

7.1 Deliverables are considered accepted if the Client does not report substantiated defects within 7 days of delivery.
7.2 Minor defects do not justify withholding acceptance or payment.

Article 8 — Intellectual property

8.1 All intellectual property rights in Deliverables remain with Noxta Studio until the Client has paid all amounts due in full.
8.2 Upon full payment, the Client receives a non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual licence to use the Deliverables for the purpose agreed in the Agreement.
8.3 Source files, design files and working files remain the property of Noxta Studio unless their transfer is expressly agreed.
8.4 Noxta Studio retains the right to use the Deliverables for its own portfolio, marketing and reference purposes unless the Client objects in writing for legitimate reasons.
8.5 Third-party assets (fonts, plugins, stock photography) are licensed to the Client subject to those third parties’ terms.
8.6 Domain names registered for the Client’s project are registered in the Client’s name from the outset, or transferred to the Client at project end without restriction or fee. Noxta Studio never holds Client domains hostage.

Article 9 — Confidentiality

Both parties treat information marked or reasonably understood as confidential with appropriate care and do not disclose it to third parties without consent, except where required by law.

Article 10 — Liability

10.1 Noxta Studio’s total liability for any direct damage is limited to the higher of (a) €5,000, or (b) the total amount paid by the Client under the relevant Agreement in the twelve months preceding the event causing the damage.
10.2 Liability for indirect damage, including but not limited to lost profits, loss of data, missed savings or business interruption, is excluded.
10.3 The limitations in this article do not apply in cases of intent or gross negligence by Noxta Studio.

Article 11 — Force majeure

Neither party is liable for failure to perform caused by force majeure, including illness, internet outages, third-party service failures, government measures or other circumstances beyond reasonable control.

Article 12 — Termination

12.1 Either party may terminate the Agreement in writing if the other party materially breaches its obligations and fails to cure within 14 days of written notice.
12.2 On termination, the Client pays for all Services performed up to the termination date plus any non-cancellable third-party costs already committed.

Article 13 — Complaints

Complaints must be submitted in writing to adrian@noxta.studio within 14 days of discovery, describing the issue as clearly as possible.

Article 14 — Applicable law and disputes

14.1 Dutch law applies to all Agreements and these conditions.
14.2 Disputes will be submitted exclusively to the competent court in the district of Zeeland-West-Brabant (location Breda), unless mandatory law designates another court.

Article 15 — Data Processing

15.1 Where the Services involve Noxta Studio processing personal data on behalf of the Client (for example: content migration, CMS administration, customer database setup, mailing-list imports), the Client is the Data Controller and Noxta Studio is the Data Processor as defined in the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679).
15.2 In such cases, the parties enter into a separate Data Processing Agreement (DPA) compliant with Art. 28 GDPR prior to processing. Noxta Studio’s standard DPA is available on request at adrian@noxta.studio.
15.3 Sub-processors used by Noxta Studio (hosting providers, analytics services, email providers, form processors) are documented in the Noxta Studio Privacy Policy at noxta.studio/privacy and may be updated from time to time with reasonable notice to the Client.

Article 16 — Amendments

Noxta Studio may amend these conditions. The version in force at the time the Agreement is formed governs that Agreement.
