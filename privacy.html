Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 May 2026

This privacy policy explains how Noxta Studio (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects your personal data when you visit noxta.studio or contact us through the website. We comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Dutch Implementation Act (UAVG).

1. Who we are

Noxta Studio is a freelance web design business based in Tilburg, the Netherlands, operated by Adrian Tajti.

2. What data we collect

2.1 Contact form

When you submit our contact form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The message you send us

2.2 Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless and does not track individuals across sites. It collects aggregated, anonymous data such as pages visited, referrer, country and device type. No personal data is processed for this purpose.

2.3 Cookies

We do not place tracking cookies. Our site may use strictly necessary cookies only if required for basic functionality.

3. Why we collect this data

PurposeDataLegal basis (GDPR Art. 6)
Responding to your enquiry Name, email, message Pre-contractual measures at your request (Art. 6(1)(b))
Improving the website Anonymous analytics Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))

4. How long we keep your data

  • Contact form submissions: kept for up to 12 months after the last contact, then deleted unless we have entered a business relationship (in which case fiscal retention rules apply — 7 years for tax-relevant records).
  • Analytics: anonymous and aggregated; retention is handled by Cloudflare under their privacy policy.

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers strictly necessary to operate the site:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — analytics and CDN/security. Cloudflare may process data outside the EU under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Email provider — for receiving and replying to your messages.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have your data deleted (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, email adrian@noxta.studio. We will respond within one month.

7. Lodging a complaint

If you believe we have not handled your data correctly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens): autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including HTTPS encryption for all site traffic and limited access to received messages.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.
