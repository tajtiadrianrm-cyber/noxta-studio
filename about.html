Six things I actually believe.

§ Manifesto
01
I stay until it works.

Most freelancers ship and vanish. I stay on call — paid retainer, occasional ad-hoc, whatever fits. Your launch day is my middle of the project.

02
Details are the work.

Generic looks generic because nobody sweated the details. I sweat them. If a button feels wrong by 2 pixels I notice — and so will your visitors.

03
No vendor talk.

I don't pad emails with corporate fluff. I tell you what I think, what I'd do, and what it'll cost. If you want a yes-man, hire someone else.

04
Full stack, one head.

Design, copy, SEO, payments, hosting, domain DNS — one person who understands all of it. No handoffs between contractors who blame each other.

05
Honest pricing.

Fixed quote after a call. If I underestimate, I eat it. If you change scope, we re-quote in writing. No hourly creep, no surprise invoices.

06
Built to hand back.

When the project ends you own everything: code, domain, logins, content. No lock-in, no leverage, no dependency on me to flip a switch.
