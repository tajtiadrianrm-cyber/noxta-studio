Quotes €120, ships 80%, vanishes when the gateway breaks. No phone number to call.

§03 · The approach

A local partner who doesn't leave until it works.

01
I stay

Retainers, not handoffs. When the payment gateway hiccups three weeks after launch, you text me. Same number, same person.

02
I do
The whole stack

Design, build, copy, SEO, payments, shipping. One person who has held every layer. Fewer handoffs, fewer holes.

03

If a button is off by two pixels, it moves. If a sentence sounds like marketing, it gets rewritten. Detail is the work.
