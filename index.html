01 / The problem

Most studios over‑promise.
Most freelancers vanish.
Most templates look like everyone else.

P / 01

Generic agencies that over‑promise.

Pitch deck full of buzzwords, three account managers, and a junior actually doing the work. The site ships late, half‑built, and the contract spells out everything they aren't responsible for.

P / 02

Offshore freelancers who disappear.

Cheap quote, broken English, and the moment the invoice clears the WhatsApp goes quiet. You're left chasing fixes for a site you don't fully understand and can't easily edit.

P / 03

Templates that look like everyone else.

Same hero. Same gradient. Same three‑icon feature row. It works, sort of, but it doesn't sell anything, because nothing about it argues for you.

02 / How I work

I treat your site like my own business.

A / 01

Sit with the brief.

Before pixels, I want to understand the customer, the order flow, the questions sales keeps repeating. The brief comes from your business, not a Notion template.

→
A / 02

One person, full stack.

Design, copy, build, payments, shipping, the email that goes out when an order ships. No handoff black holes. One inbox, one head.

→
A / 03

Don't leave until it works.

Launch is not the end. I stay through the first month of real traffic, real orders, real questions, and fix what reality finds. That's where most freelancers exit.

→
A / 04

Build it so you can run it.

Clean structure, plain‑language docs, clear admin. You shouldn't need me to add a product or change a phone number, only when you want me to.
