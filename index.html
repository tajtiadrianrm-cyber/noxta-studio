03 · How I work

How this usually goes.

/01

I stay until it works.

Lots of freelancers ship and disappear. I don't. Retainer or ad-hoc, you can reach me after launch. That's usually when the real work starts anyway.

/02

Details are the work.

Generic sites look generic because nobody sweated the details. If a button is off by two pixels I'll catch it. So will your visitors, even if they can't quite say why something feels off.

/03

Yours to keep.

When the project ends you own all of it: the code, the domain, the logins, the content. If you decide to take it somewhere else later, I'll help you move.
