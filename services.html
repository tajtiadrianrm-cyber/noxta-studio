§ FAQ

Things I get
asked weekly.

Do you do hosting?
I set it up on Hostinger (or your provider of choice) and configure DNS. The hosting bill stays in your name — I never hold your domain hostage.
What if I want to edit it myself?
Encouraged. I build on WordPress or Shopify so you can. I leave a one-page handover doc and walk you through updates over a call.
Do you work outside the Netherlands?
Yes. Half of my hours go to remote clients. Tilburg-based clients get the option of in-person kickoff and review meetings.
What's NOT in scope?
Logo iconography from scratch, photography, video, and ongoing ad campaigns. SEO setup yes; SEO content marketing no.
Can I start small and scale?
That's the recommended path. A Tier I landing page now, a Tier II site in six months, a maintenance retainer when you have more to lose.
Do you take rush jobs?
Occasionally, +30% surcharge. Email me with your timeline.
