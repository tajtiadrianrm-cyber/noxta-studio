§01 · Challenge

Four thousand parts. No store.

Inventory in a shed. Knowledge in one head.

Alex had spent decades collecting and rebuilding parts for vintage outboard engines — Johnson, Evinrude, Mercury — most of which the original manufacturers stopped making before the iPhone existed. Local buyers came and went; collectors in Germany, the Netherlands and France wanted to order online; nothing existed online.

§02 · Approach

Six decisions, one shop.

Each one chosen for a reason.
StackWordPress + WooCommerce on Flatsome — fast to ship, easy for Alex to run himself, easy for me to maintain.
IdentityDark vintage — ink, brass, rust. Serif headlines, monospace specs. Anti-template. Felt like the trade, not the platform.
SearchFiboSearch, tuned for SKU + part-number indexing. The first thing a buyer types is a number, not a noun.
PaymentsWooCommerce Payments with Bancontact, iDEAL, card. Belgium and NL buyers expected local rails — anything else is friction.
SEORank Math + schema + sitemap. Engine-model pages structured so Google can map a 1972 Johnson 50 to twelve parts in two clicks.
Mobile UXCustom mobile search experience — most queries come from a phone in a workshop. Big tap targets, persistent search bar.
